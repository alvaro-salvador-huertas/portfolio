Evidence & limits

Four real reviews of prepared candidates: two defects detected beyond the available tests and two justified approvals. The findings were checked automatically by Codex.

In a separate experiment, two small exercises were approved on the first attempt: four real agent calls across development and review.

153 automated tests with simulated agents check the controller, not model quality.

These controlled cases do not establish general reliability. A second repair attempt is implemented and covered by simulations, but has not been observed with the real model.
